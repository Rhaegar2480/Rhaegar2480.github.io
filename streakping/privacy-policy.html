Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Thank you for using StreakPing (package name: com.androidapkent.streakping). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StreakPing handles information on your device and what third-party services are used.

1. Local-First Data Storage

StreakPing does not require account registration and does not operate any backend servers to store your personal data. All app configurations, tracked app selections, daily reset hours, reminder schedules, and check-in history are stored locally on your device.

2. Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • Launchable Apps Query (MAIN / LAUNCHER): Used strictly on your device to display a list of launchable apps so you can choose which apps to track and open them in one tap. Your list of installed apps is never uploaded or shared.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to deliver your scheduled daily check-in reminders.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS — Optional): Only used if you explicitly grant Usage Access in Android Settings and select the Auto (UsageStats) check-in mode for a tracked app. StreakPing checks locally on-device whether that specific tracked app was used in the foreground for the configured duration. Usage statistics are never transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

StreakPing is provided free of charge and displays banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information (such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, device model, and ad interaction metrics) to serve and measure ads.

For more details on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Data Backup & Deletion

You can export or import your local configuration using the JSON backup feature in Settings. You can delete any tracked app inside the app at any time, or uninstall StreakPing to permanently remove all locally stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

StreakPing is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: androdiapkent@gmail.com

隱私權政策（Privacy Policy）

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

感謝您使用 StreakPing（套件名稱：com.androidapkent.streakping）。 我們非常重視您的隱私，本隱私權政策說明本應用程式如何在您的裝置上處理資訊，以及所使用之第三方服務。

1. 本地優先儲存（Local-First）

StreakPing 不需要註冊帳號，亦不設有任何後端伺服器來儲存您的個人資料。 所有的追蹤 App 清單、每日重置時間、提醒排程與簽到紀錄皆僅儲存於您的裝置本機。

2. 權限使用與本機處理說明

  • 可啟動應用程式查詢（MAIN / LAUNCHER）：僅於本機讀取可啟動的 App 清單， 讓您選擇要追蹤的目標並一鍵開啟。我們絕不會上傳或分享您手機內已安裝的 App 清單。
  • 通知與精準鬧鐘（POST_NOTIFICATIONS、SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM）： 僅用於準時發送您所設定的每日簽到提醒。
  • 使用情況存取權（PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS — 選用）：僅在您主動於 Android 系統設定授權， 且對特定 App 選擇「自動偵測 (UsageStats)」模式時才會使用。本 App 僅於本機確認該目標 App 今日前景停留時間是否達標， 相關使用紀錄絕不會傳送至裝置外部。

3. 第三方廣告服務（Google AdMob）

StreakPing 採免費提供並透過 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅廣告。Google AdMob 可能會依其規範蒐集並處理特定裝置資訊 （例如 Android 廣告識別碼、IP 位址、裝置型號與廣告互動數據），以提供與衡量廣告成效。

如需進一步了解 Google 如何處理資料，請參閱 Google 隱私權政策 與 Google 如何使用來自使用我們服務的網站或應用程式的資訊。

4. 資料備份與刪除

您可隨時透過設定頁的 JSON 備份功能匯出或匯入本機設定，亦可在 App 內刪除任一追蹤項目，或直接解除安裝 StreakPing 以永久移除所有本機資料。

5. 兒童隱私

StreakPing 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集兒童之個人資訊。
