Privacy Policy — FindMyCar

Application: FindMyCar (com.androidapkent.findmycar)
Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Contact: androidapkent@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how FindMyCar ("the App", developed by Kent) handles user information and device permissions. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your personal data safe.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

FindMyCar is designed as an offline-first utility. The following data you create or capture is stored solely on your local device and is never uploaded to or collected by the developer's servers:

  • Parking Records & Locations: GPS coordinates, floor levels, pillar/spot numbers, parking timestamps, and fee presets.
  • Vehicle Profiles: Vehicle nicknames, optional license plate numbers, vehicle types, and color tags.
  • Photos: Pictures of your parking spot captured via the Camera or selected from your Gallery are saved inside the app's private local storage.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used only when you record or view a parking spot to center the map on your current position and calculate walking distance to your vehicle. Location data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used only when you choose to take a photo of your parking spot or nearby pillars.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used only when you enable Parking Reminders or Payment Reminders to alert you at your chosen time.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to load map tiles from OpenStreetMap and display advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

The App integrates the following third-party services, which may collect or process limited technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • OpenStreetMap (OSMDroid): Used to render map tiles. Map tile requests may transmit your IP address and requested map region to OpenStreetMap tile servers. (OSMF Privacy Policy)
  • Google Maps App (External Intent): When you tap "Navigate", the App opens Google Maps on your device with your saved destination coordinates.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your parking records, vehicle data, and photos are stored locally on your device, you can delete individual records or clear cached photos anytime inside the App, or remove all local app data by uninstalling FindMyCar.

5. Children's Privacy

FindMyCar is a general utility app for drivers and riders and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at androidapkent@gmail.com.

生效日期： 2026 年 11 月 19 日
聯絡信箱： androidapkent@gmail.com

本隱私權政策說明 FindMyCar (尋車幫手)（以下簡稱「本 App」）如何處理您的裝置權限與資料。我們高度重視您的隱私，所有個人停車與車輛資料皆以本機優先儲存。

1. 儲存於您裝置本地的資料

本 App 不設開發者遠端伺服器，以下您輸入或拍攝的資料僅儲存於您的手機本機內，不會上傳至開發者伺服器：

  • 停車紀錄與位置：停車經緯度座標、樓層、柱號／車位號、停車時間與費率範本。
  • 車輛資訊：車輛暱稱、車牌號碼（選填）、車種型態與顏色標籤。
  • 現場照片：您透過相機拍攝或從相簿選取的車位照片，僅保存於 App 本地私有目錄。

2. 裝置權限使用說明

  • 位置權限（精確／概略位置）：僅用於記錄停車時自動定位地圖中心，以及計算您目前位置與車輛之間的距離。
  • 相機權限：僅於您主動點擊拍照記錄車位周遭環境時使用。
  • 通知與精準鬧鐘權限：僅於您手動開啟「停車提醒」或「繳費通知提醒」時，於設定時間發出通知。
  • 網路存取權限：用於載入 OpenStreetMap 地圖圖資及透過 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅廣告。

3. 第三方服務與資料分享說明

本 App 使用以下第三方服務，該等服務可能依其各自隱私權政策處理必要之技術識別資料：

  • Google AdMob：用於顯示應用程式內橫幅廣告。AdMob 可能存取裝置廣告識別碼 (Advertising ID)、IP 位址與廣告互動資訊，以提供廣告投放與防止詐欺。 （Google 隱私權政策）
  • OpenStreetMap (OSMDroid)：用於顯示地圖圖資，下載圖磚時會向圖磚伺服器傳送標準網路請求（含 IP 位址與地圖範圍）。 （OSMF 隱私權政策）
  • Google Maps 外部導航：點擊「導航」時會將目的地座標傳遞給您手機上的 Google Maps App 進行步行導航。

4. 資料保留與刪除

您可隨時於 App 內刪除單筆停車紀錄、清除照片快取，或直接解除安裝本 App 以完整移除所有本機資料。

5. 聯絡我們

若您對本隱私權政策有任何疑問，請來信至： androidapkent@gmail.com。
